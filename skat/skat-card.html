Skat

Three players · 32 cards · 10 each + 2 Skat
120 card points · declarer needs 61

Setup

Deal in packets, clockwise from Forehand: 3 each — 2 to Skat — 4 each — 3 each.

Declarer plays alone against two defenders.

Trumps & rank

Jacks are ALWAYS trump, and a Jack is never its face suit:

J♣ > J♠ > J♥ > J♦

Suit game (say ♣)

J♣ J♠ J♥ J♦ · A 10 K Q 9 8 7 of ♣ = 11 trumps.
Other suits rank A 10 K Q 9 8 7.

Grand

Only the four Jacks are trump.

Null

No trump at all, and the rank order flips to 7 8 9 10 J Q K A. Declarer must take zero tricks.

Multiplier

matadors + 1 (for the game) + 1 for Hand + 1 per bonus (Schneider, Schwarz).

Hand = you played without picking up the Skat.

Matadors

The unbroken run of top trumps from J♣ downward.

Hold J♣“with N” — how many in a row you hold
Miss J♣“without N” — how many in a row you lack

Same multiplier either way. A big “without” means the top trumps are AGAINST you — a weak hand with a high paper value.

Clubs, with J♣ J♠ J♥ = with 3 → 12 × 4 = 48

Clubs, missing J♣ = without 1 → 12 × 2 = 24

Bidding

Numbers are game values: 18, 20, 22, 23, 24, 27, 30, 33, 36, 40, … You name a game only after winning the auction.

Middlehand bids to Forehand; the survivor bids to Rearhand. On a tie the holder wins — Forehand > Middlehand > Rearhand.

Safe ceiling = base × (matadors + 1). Bid to here freely; go past it only if a Schneider is a lock.

⚠ Overbid = automatic loss. If your final game value is below your bid you lose no matter how many points you took. The Skat can raise or lower your matadors.

Open the bidding when

You hold J♣ or J♠ + a five-card trump suit (or Jacks and Aces for Grand) + a side Ace.
No Jacks ≈ pass.

Play basics

Follow suit if you can; otherwise play anything. A Jack counts as trump, not as its face suit.

Highest trump wins; with no trump, the highest card of the led suit.

Forehand leads first; the winner of each trick leads the next.

Master card = every higher card is gone. A King is not a master until the Ace AND the Ten have appeared.

Declarer — offence

  1. Discard into the Skat to bank points and make a void.
  2. Draw trumps first — lead a low Jack to flush a higher one — then cash Aces.
  3. Run your winners. Count trumps down so you know when the side suits are safe.
  4. Never overbid, and don't cash a side Ace while a defender can still ruff.

Defenders — one team, target 60

  1. Smear — pile high cards onto your partner's winning tricks.
  2. Starve — play your cheapest card onto the declarer's winning tricks. Never feed an A/10/K where a 7/8/9 will do. Applies to discards too.
  3. Don't lead into his Aces or voids. Make the declarer lead his own long suits.
  4. Lead trumps when you are trump-strong; don't when you're weak.
  5. Cash your masters the moment the declarer is out of trumps.
  6. Track voids — once he ruffs a suit, stop leading it.

Two-question lead check

  1. Do I hold a master here? (every higher card gone) → cash it.
  2. If not, has the Ace of this suit appeared? If no → don't lead into it.

Count in layers

Build one layer at a time:

Jacks (4) → trumps (11 / 4 / 0)
→ Aces & Tens → running points

Count down, remember voids, and update once per trick. You already see your own ten cards — subtract them first.
